Learner Demographics and Course Enrollment Behavior Analysis on EduPro
Detailed guide and project requirements for the Learner Demographics and Course Enrollment Behavior Analysis on EduPro analysis.

Background and Context:
Online learning platforms serve diverse learners with varying:

• Age groups
• Gender distributions
• Learning goals
• Subject interests
For a platform like EduPro, understanding who the learners are and how they choose courses is foundational for:

• Designing relevant courses
• Improving learner engagement
• Targeted marketing and outreach
• Enhancing inclusivity and accessibility
This project focuses on descriptive learner intelligence, not prediction or monetization.

Unified Mentor
Toronto Government Parks, Forestry & Recreation
Problem Statement
Despite having user and transaction data, EduPro currently lacks clear answers to:

• Which age groups are most active on the platform?
• How do enrollment patterns differ by gender?
• What course categories are preferred by different learner segments?
• Are beginner, intermediate, or advanced courses more popular among specific age groups?
Without these insights, decisions related to course creation, marketing, and platform growth remain intuition-driven rather than data-driven.

Key Analytical Questions
This project aims to answer:

• What is the age distribution of learners on EduPro?
• How does course enrollment vary across age groups?
• Are there gender-based differences in course selection?
• Which course categories attract the highest enrollments?
• Do beginners prefer certain course types or levels?
Analytical Methodology (Step-by-Step)
Data Integration
• Join Users ↔ Transactions ↔ Courses using UserID and CourseID
• Ensure data consistency and referential integrity
Learner Demographic Analysis
• Segment learners into age bands (e.g., <18, 18–25, 26–35, 36–45, 45+)
• Analyze gender distribution across the platform
• Measure participation levels per demographic group
Enrollment Distribution Analysis
• Count enrollments by:
○ CourseCategory
○ CourseType
○ CourseLevel
• Identify most and least popular categories
Demographics × Course Preference Analysis
• Age group vs course category heatmaps
• Gender vs course level comparisons
• Identify learner segments and their preferences
Behavioral Insights
• Average courses taken per learner
• Enrollment concentration among active users
• Beginner vs advanced learner behavior patterns

Conclusion
This project provides foundational learner intelligence for the EduPro platform. By systematically analyzing who the learners are and how they enroll, EduPro can move toward data-driven education planning, ensuring its offerings align with learner needs, preferences, and diversity.
